Interpretation boundary

Results describe the recorded configuration and dataset split only. Development and validation-subset runs are not official BSDS500 test-set benchmark claims. Boundary F1 is the documented MonoGrowLab tolerance metric, not the official BSDS500 boundary benchmark implementation. For asymmetric families such as brightness scaling, severity-level rows preserve direction-specific effects; the family-level mean is only the configured average across darkening and brightening conditions.
